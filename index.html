The $PEPES Manifesto

THE FROG
NEVER DIES.

Every cycle, the charts change. The coins change. The narratives change. One thing never does — the frog.

Pepe has outlived every trend, every crash and every “memes are over” tweet. Born in a comic, raised by the internet, he became the most remixed face online — smug, sad, rare, cosmic, pixelated, painted on canvas and printed on hoodies. Every remix is proof of the same thing: culture can’t be rugged.

$PEPES is that spirit, multiplied. Not one Pepe — every Pepe. A token for the people who make them, collect them and post them at 3 a.m. while the timeline sleeps. PEPE.PICS is its home: a gallery where every picture is a chapter of the story, and every holder writes the next one.

Hype fades. Memes that make people smile survive every season. That’s the only edge that lasts.
